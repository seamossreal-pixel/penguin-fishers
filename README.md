# Penguin Fishers

A chaotic co-op fishing game for 1-6 penguins. Catch fish, keep the Walrus boss happy, fight a giant fish and sail to new islands together, on PC or phone.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/seamossreal-pixel/penguen-balikcilar/releases/latest)**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PenguenBalikcilar.zip` | PC (Windows) package: launcher + game. Unzip it and run `Penguen Balikcilar.exe`. |
| `PenguenBalikcilar.apk` | Android phone version. |
| `PenguenLauncher-N.exe` | Launcher only. It downloads and updates the game by itself. |

The launcher and the game check this page for new versions. On PC the game offers **Update** and the launcher installs it; on Android the game downloads the new APK and opens the installer.

## How to play

1. **Fish.** Hold **left click** and release to cast. When the bobber sinks, click to hook the fish, then keep it inside the slippery green bar until it's reeled in.
2. **Sell.** Carry the fish to the Walrus's bucket and press **E** to sell it. Fill the daily quota; everything you sell after the quota goes to the team wallet. There is no clock: when the quota is met, the host ends the day.
3. **Walrus orders.** The Walrus orders a specific fish with a deadline (shown at the top of the screen). Bring it in time for a big bonus. Miss it and he gets off his throne, charges at you and slaps you into next week. His **patience** (3 strikes, top bar) drops with every missed order, junk delivery and slap; run out before the quota is met and you're fired. Delivered orders win some patience back.
4. **Shop.** Six rods (Bamboo, Carbon, Golden, Crystal, Lava, Rainbow), steel line, flippers, scarves and baits at Seal Fikri's market.
5. **Boss.** Throw the boss bait into the whirlpool. Reel the giant fish ashore, then **punch it (F)** while it's stunned. It drops a gold bar.
6. **Sail.** Give the gold to Captain Pelican, board the boat and follow the compass to the next island. Every island is different: Glacier, Palm and Volcano islands each have their own look, fish bonuses and two fish you can only catch there (Ice Cod, Crystal Fish, Clownfish, Parrotfish, Coalfish, Lava Fish).

Also on the island:
- **Levels.** Earn XP for almost everything; level up to unlock new hats, glasses and colours.
- **Surprises.** Treasure chests wash ashore and fish sometimes rain from the sky. Random day events: seagull invasion, fog, storm, moon gravity, ice skating, fish rain, golden day and the Walrus Feast Day (faster orders, double pay).
- **Continue.** Every won day is saved on the host's PC; press CONTINUE in the lobby to pick up where you left off.
- **Hunger.** Cook a fish at the campfire (cooking minigame) and eat it (**X**).
- **Seagulls** steal fish (but never while you cook). Pick up stones (**E** at a stone pile) and throw them (**G**, auto-aim) at gulls, or at your friends for a bonk (4 s cooldown).
- **Fish index** (**I**) lists every fish you've discovered.
- **Wardrobe.** Hats, glasses, bow ties and colours for your penguin.
- **Accounts and friends.** Make an account, add friends by tag and join their room in one click.
- Secrets. A few of them.

## Controls (PC)

New players get short one-time tips in the corner, and **ESC** shows the full controls list.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left click | Cast / hook / reel |
| Right click | Reel back |
| Q | Throw what you're carrying |
| E | Pick up / drop / sell at the bucket / talk / cook / board and leave the boat |
| F | Slap (and punch the boss) |
| G | Throw a stone |
| X | Eat |
| I | Fish index |
| Ctrl | Slide |
| 1-9 | Honk and dances |
| V | Push-to-talk voice chat |
| Hold R | Back to the stall |

## Playing with friends

Press **CREATE ROOM (online)** and send the room code to your friends, or add them as friends and press **JOIN** next to their name. LAN and solo play work too.

The game is in English by default. You can switch to Turkish (Türkçe) from the menu.
