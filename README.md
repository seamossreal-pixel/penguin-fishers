# Penguin Fishers

A chaotic co-op fishing game for 1-6 penguins. Catch fish, keep the Walrus boss happy, fight a giant fish and sail to new islands together, on PC or phone.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/seamossreal-pixel/penguen-balikcilar/releases/latest)**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PenguenBalikcilar.zip` | PC (Windows) package: launcher + game. Unzip it and run `Penguen Balikcilar.exe`. |
| `PenguenBalikcilar.apk` | Android phone version. |
| `PenguenLauncher-N.exe` | Launcher only. It downloads and updates the game by itself. |

The launcher and the game check this page for new versions. On PC the game offers **Update** and the launcher installs it. On Android the game opens with its own launcher screen: it checks for a new version, downloads the APK by itself and opens the installer.

## How to play

1. **Fish.** Hold **left click** and release to cast. When the bobber sinks, click to hook the fish, then keep it inside the slippery green bar until it's reeled in.
2. **Sell.** Carry the fish to the Walrus's bucket and press **E** to sell it. Fill the daily quota; everything you sell after the quota goes to the team wallet. There is no clock: when the quota is met, the host ends the day.
3. **Walrus orders.** The Walrus sits on his throne at the far end of the island and orders a specific fish (shown at the top of the screen and above his head). There is no deadline, and the fish he wants bites more often. Bring it for a big bonus. Junk and slaps cost his **patience** (3 strikes, top bar); run out before the quota is met and you're fired. Delivered orders win some patience back.
4. **Market.** Seal Fikri's market has stands with the goods on display: walk up to one and press **E** to buy. Six rods (Bamboo, Carbon, Golden, Crystal, Lava, Rainbow), steel line, flippers, scarves and baits, plus one special item per island: gull repellent (Home), warm scarf (Glacier), a lucky parrot (Palm) and magma bait (Volcano).
5. **Boss.** Throw the boss bait into the whirlpool. Reel the giant fish ashore, then **punch it (F)** while it's stunned. It drops a gold bar.
6. **Sail.** Give the gold to Captain Pelican, board the boat and follow the compass to the next island. Every island is different: Glacier, Palm and Volcano islands each have their own trees, decorations, Walrus outfit, fish bonuses and two fish you can only catch there (Ice Cod, Crystal Fish, Clownfish, Parrotfish, Coalfish, Lava Fish). When a day ends nobody is teleported: keep doing what you were doing.

Also on the island:
- **Levels.** Earn XP for almost everything; level up to unlock new hats, glasses and colours.
- **Surprises.** Treasure chests wash ashore and fish sometimes rain from the sky. Random day events: seagull invasion, fog, storm, moon gravity, ice skating, fish rain, golden day and the Walrus Feast Day (faster orders, double pay).
- **Moon Day FFA.** A party mode from the lobby: low gravity, unlimited stones and an extra-strong punch. Knock your friends into the sea; the first penguin to fall in and freeze loses the round. The host picks the number of rounds.
- **Bag.** 4 inventory slots: press **Z** to store what you carry or take it back, scroll to pick a slot.
- **Continue.** Every won day is saved on the host's PC; press CONTINUE in the lobby to pick up where you left off.
- **Hunger.** Cook a fish at the campfire (cooking minigame) and eat it (**X**).
- **Seagulls** steal fish (but never while you cook), and they can even carry off a frozen penguin, flying low over the sea. Pick up stones (**E** at a stone pile) and throw them (**G**, auto-aim) at gulls, or at your friends for a bonk (4 s cooldown).
- **Fish index** (**I**) lists every fish you've discovered.
- **Wardrobe.** Hats, glasses, bow ties and colours for your penguin.
- **Accounts and friends.** Make an account, add friends by tag and join their room in one click.
- Secrets. A few of them.

## Controls (PC)

New players get short one-time tips in the corner, and **ESC** shows the full controls list. The camera position (X/Y/Z) can be moved in Settings.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left click | Cast / hook / reel |
| Right click | Reel back |
| Q | Throw what you're carrying |
| E | Pick up / drop / sell at the bucket / talk / cook / board and leave the boat |
| F | Slap (and punch the boss) |
| G | Throw a stone |
| X | Eat |
| Z | Bag: store / take (mouse wheel picks the slot) |
| I | Fish index |
| Ctrl | Slide |
| 1-9 | Honk and dances |
| V | Push-to-talk voice chat |
| Hold R | Back to the stall |

## Playing with friends

Press **CREATE ROOM (online)** and send the room code to your friends, or add them as friends and press **JOIN** next to their name. LAN and solo play work too.

The game is in English by default. You can switch to Turkish (Türkçe) from the menu.
