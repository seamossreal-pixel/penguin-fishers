# Penguin Fishers

A chaotic co-op fishing game for 1-6 penguins. Catch fish, keep the Walrus boss happy, fight a giant whale, sail to new islands together and finally fly a rocket to **the Moon**, on PC or phone.

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/seamossreal-pixel/penguin-fishers/releases/latest)**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PenguenBalikcilar.zip` | PC (Windows) game. Unzip it and run `PenguenBalikcilar.exe` inside the `PenguenBalikcilar` folder. |
| `PenguenBalikcilar.apk` | Android phone version. |
| `Game.zip` | The PC game files (used by the in-game updater). |

The game checks this page for new versions. On PC the main menu offers **Update**: the game downloads the new version itself, closes, installs it and starts again (no launcher since 1.27.1; an old launcher you still have keeps working). On Android the game opens with its own launcher screen: it checks for a new version, downloads the APK by itself and opens the installer.

## How to play

1. **Fish.** Hold **left click** and release to cast. When the bobber sinks, click to hook the fish, then keep it inside the slippery green bar until it's reeled in.
2. **Sell.** Carry the fish to Boss Walrus and press **E**: it flies into his mouth, he gulps it down and coins fountain straight back to you (since 1.19 there is no bucket and no orders, he pays for every fish on the spot). Since 1.25 one press sells the fish in your hand **and every raw fish in your bag**, one after another. Fill the daily quota; everything you sell after the quota goes to the team wallet. There is no clock: when the quota is met, the host ends the day.
3. **The Walrus.** Boss Walrus sits on his throne at the far end of the island; stand on the golden ring in front of him to sell, or just throw the fish at him. Junk and slaps cost his **patience** (3 strikes, top bar); run out before the quota is met and you're fired.
4. **Market.** Seal Fikri's big hut faces the campfire and every product hangs on its front wall: look at one to see its name and price (red if you cannot afford it) and press **E** to buy. Six rods (Bamboo, Carbon, Golden, Crystal, Lava, Rainbow), steel line, flippers, scarves and baits, plus one special item per island: gull repellent (Home), warm scarf (Glacier), a lucky parrot (Palm) and magma bait (Volcano).
5. **Boss.** Throw the boss bait into the sea (since 1.27 anywhere in deep water; the boss rises where it lands, the whirlpool still works). Reel the giant fish ashore, then **punch it (F)** while it's stunned. Bosses hit hard and fight back: at half health every boss gets **enraged**: it breaks free, flees to the water and attacks faster and in combos, so you have to hook it again. Every island has its own boss with its own moves: the Blue Whale (Home, since 1.26: it spouts water, makes waves and tail-slaps), Frost Narwhal (Glacier), Coconut Crab King (Palm), Magma Eel (Volcano), Bullfrog King (Swamp) and the Ghost Pirate Octopus (Pirate Cove). Each one drops its own loot: a gold bar, a frost crystal, a magma core, a frog crown, a doubloon chest... Casting at a boss is easy: throw roughly toward it and the line goes to it (aim assist, 1.26). A boss that waits too long gets bored, says goodbye and dives away.
6. **Sail.** Give the boss loot to Captain Pelican: the next island appears on the horizon and you get a boat. Sail there together and the crew lands on the beach (the boat is a rental, it sinks behind you, so every island needs a new boss and a new boat). Every island is different: Glacier, Palm, Volcano, Mushroom Swamp and Pirate Cove each have their own climate, mountain peak, trees, Walrus outfit, fish bonuses, market item and fish you can only catch there.

Every island also has its own playground (the islets were removed in 1.29):

| Island | Playground | Islet |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Trampolines | Lighthouse |
| Glacier | Ice rink (slide!) and a snow ramp into the sea | Ice arch with a frozen fish |
| Palm | Dive board and beach balls | Tiki hut with a hammock |
| Volcano | Steam geysers that launch you into the sky | Obsidian shrine |
| Mushroom Swamp | Bouncy giant mushrooms and a lily pad path | Witch hut |
| Pirate Cove | Walk the plank, barrel cannon across the island | Shipwreck |
| Candy Island (1.27) | Jelly trampolines, cake mountain, lollipop and candy cane trees | Gingerbread house |
| Desert Oasis (1.27) | Sand geysers, cacti, a ring-shaped oasis | Tiny pyramid |
| Crystal Isle (1.27) | Crystal rink, glowing crystal trees; the boss drops the Moon Crystal for the **rocket** | Giant crystal |
| **The Moon** (1.27) | Low gravity, trampolines that throw you into space, stars and the Earth in the sky | Lunar lander |

**To the Moon (1.27).** On Crystal Isle Captain Pelican sells a **rocket** instead of a boat. Board it, the pilot presses forward: 3, 2, 1, liftoff, with a cinematic launch and a landing on the Moon. The Moon's boss drops Moon Cheese, which buys the rocket home to Earth. The new islands' bosses: Gummy Frog King, Pharaoh Crab, Crystal Narwhal and Alien Octopus.

**Things to do on every island (1.27):** a lucky wheel (coins, fish, a banana... or a BOING), a dance floor with a disco ball (everyone near dances), a hammock (refills health and energy) and a telescope on the summit. The Home islet's **lighthouse can be entered**: radio, bed, treasure chest, lamp switch, a ladder to the balcony with a telescope, a foghorn and a dive board into the sea. The volcano erupts every minute: ash clouds, a lava fountain, lava bombs and lava rivers that flow down to the sea and cool (don't stand in them).


Each islet has a giant clam: open it with **E** for 3 pearls (once per island).

Also on the island:
- **Levels.** Earn XP for almost everything; level up to unlock new hats, glasses and colours.
- **Surprises.** Treasure chests wash ashore and fish sometimes rain from the sky. Random day events: seagull invasion, fog, storm, moon gravity, ice skating, fish rain, golden day and the Walrus Feast Day (every fish pays +50%).
- **Hotbar.** 6 slots at the bottom: the rod (1) and 5 bag slots (2-6) for fish, items and your stones (stones take a slot only while you have some). Press the key of the selected slot again to go bare-handed: fists out, click or **F** to punch.
- **Health and going down.** At 0 hunger you lose 10 health every second; the health bar only shows when you are hurt. At 0 health you collapse (1.19): you lie on the ground for 25 s while a teammate can run up and **hold E** for 2.5 s to pick you up with 50 health. If nobody does, you die and respawn at the campfire, and the next collapse lasts 5 s longer (30, 35...).
- **The Gull King.** Buy a Royal Firework at the market and light it (X): a giant crowned gull attacks with dives and egg bombs and calls its guards. Only stones hurt it, and it dodges while it circles high up: hit it while it dives! It drops the Golden Gull Wing (glide while carrying it, sells for a fortune).
- **Buried treasure.** On Pirate Cove the Treasure Map comes with a shovel: red X marks appear in the sand, dig there (X) for chests.
- **Every island has its own shape:** a snowy mountain on Glacier, a volcano with a lava crater, an atoll lagoon on Palm, ponds in the swamp, a rocky cove on Pirate Cove.
- **Real grass** that sways in the wind and bends around penguins (Settings > Graphics > Grass: off / low / high).
- **Grab your friends.** Press **E** next to a friend to lift them over your head, **Q** to throw them (into the sea, onto a trampoline...) or **E** to put them down. The carried penguin mashes **Space** to wriggle free. Frozen friends can be carried to the campfire to thaw.
- **Fish revolt.** Hold a fish too long and it fights back: block its tail slaps left or right in time, or it slips away into the sea.
- **Comedy.** Slap people with a fish, talking fish with last words, belly-slide bowling, drumroll fake-outs, faceplant judges, gravestones with epitaphs and more.
- **Mutations.** Fish sometimes come out **Silver**, **Gold**, **Shocked** or **Diamond**: they sparkle or crackle and sell for 1.5x to 5x.
- **Rod skills.** Better rods have skills: Quick Hook (Carbon), Midas Touch (Golden), Clear Sight (Crystal), Hot Catch (Lava), Rainbow Luck (Rainbow).
- **Snack machine.** Next to the campfire in the middle of the island: energy drink, fireworks (scare gulls), chum bucket (fish bite faster), hot cocoa (no freezing), banana peel (friends slip!), golden lure and mystery box. Press **X** to use.
- **Pearl shop.** Earn pearls by levelling up, catching mutated fish, filling the quota and opening giant clams; spend them on rod skins, stone skins, trails and special hats. Every cosmetic has its own effect: rod tips sparkle, hats glow and stones leave a trail. Pearls are earned in game. You can also support the game with a donation (DONATE button in the main menu) and get a pearl code; enter it in Pearl Shop > BUY PEARLS. The Aura Crown costs 2500 pearls, for when you really want to gain aura.
- **Walrus revenge.** Slap the Walrus and he gets angry, charges and slaps you into the sky.
- **Emote wheel.** Hold **B** and push the mouse toward a dance; the wheel shows a preview of the dance.
- **Continue.** Every won day is saved on the host's PC; press CONTINUE in the lobby to pick up where you left off.
- **Hunger.** Cook a fish at the campfire and eat it (**X**). Since 1.26 cooking is simple: hold fire to lower the fish toward the flames, release to lift it, keep it in the green (yellow = perfect); a big line always tells you what to do and the fish flips itself halfway.
- **Cutscenes (1.26).** Short cinematic scenes when you join, at the start of a shift, when you reach a new island, when a boss appears or is defeated, at the end of a day and when you get fired. Skip with **E** / **Space** (SKIP on phones) or turn them off in Settings.
- **Tap the prompt (1.27).** Tap (phone) or click the big [E] prompt to use it. On phones the mini-games (reeling, cooking, the gull tug) use their own green button, so you never throw your cooked fish by accident.
- **Prompts (1.26).** Walk up to the campfire, the Walrus, the shop, Captain Pelican or the boat and a big **[E] COOK / SELL / SHOP / TALK / BOARD** prompt shows what to do. The team money is shown big in the top bar (sales go to the quota first, money grows after the quota is met).
- **Fits every screen (1.26).** The UI adapts to every phone, including foldables (Galaxy Z Fold inner and cover screens), tablets, older 16:9 phones and notches. Phone buttons that do nothing right now are hidden.
- **Seagulls** steal fish (but never while you cook), and they can even carry off a frozen penguin, flying low over the sea. Pick up stones (**E** at a stone pile) and throw them (**G**, auto-aim) at gulls, or at your friends for a bonk (4 s cooldown).
- **Fish index** (**I**) lists every fish you've discovered. Version 1.17 added 8 new fish, like the Main Character Fish, No Cap Cod, the Delulu Fish and the Lag Fish.
- **Wardrobe.** Hats, glasses, bow ties and colours for your penguin.
- **Accounts and friends.** Make an account, add friends by tag and join their room in one click. The second tab of the friends screen lists every player alphabetically.
- **Music that reacts.** The sea shanty gets drums while you fish and reel, and a full battle band when a boss shows up (even louder when it's enraged).
- **Feel.** Big hits freeze the screen for a split second, landings squash your penguin; camera shake can be set to Off / Low / Full in Settings.
- **Camera.** Since 1.26 the default is a close third-person camera. Press **T** for first person: your two flippers are on screen, the right one holds the rod and they waddle, slap, throw and paddle with you (also in Settings). Head bob is off by default and can be turned on in Settings.
- **A goofy new penguin (1.24).** The character is a chubby, slightly dumb penguin made in Blender: wonky eyes, a sleepy eyelid and a beak that hangs open. Its eyes follow the nearest character (friends, the Walrus, Seal Fikri, Captain Pelican), it walks with bending knees, runs bent forward with its flippers thrown back, holds the rod in its right hand, cranks the reel with the left flipper and scratches its head when the fish take too long. Hats, glasses and scarves fit the new head. In 1.25 its flippers rest at its sides and swing with its steps when it runs.
- **Being a penguin.** Flap your flippers in the air (penguins can't fly, but it helps a little; flap right before hitting the ground after a long fall for a **clutch** landing), leap out of the water like a dolphin while swimming fast, and huddle next to a friend to refill your stamina.
- **Ragdolls everywhere.** Fall from high up (lower threshold since 1.19), run or belly-slide into a wall and you flop over; press **Y** to go limp whenever you want. The camera pulls back so you can watch yourself flop.
- **Feedback.** Found a bug or want a feature? Press **Esc** and pick FEEDBACK (or use the button at the bottom left of the main menu), write a short note and it goes straight to the developer.
- **Sell prompt.** Carry a fish near Boss Walrus and a big **[E] SELL** prompt appears above him; from further away an arrow shows the distance.
- **Don't dig here!** On Pirate Cove the DON'T DIG HERE signs are an invitation: dig under them with the shovel for a chest... or a surprise.
- **Selling feels good.** Sold fish fly from your flippers into the Walrus's open mouth (CHOMP), coins fountain out of it to you with a real cash-register ka-ching, and the money floats up into the quota bar.
- **Every catch has its own hold (1.19).** Pufferfish and crabs are held at arm's length, eels become a scarf, the brick phone goes to your ear, bottles are raised for a toast, ducks ride on your head, some fish dangle by the tail, some are cradled like a baby or pointed like a sword. Your third-person penguin now has the same blade flippers you see in first person, and carried items follow your view so they never float.
- **The boat (1.19).** A proper fishing boat with a walkable deck, a wheelhouse, benches and a bow lookout: get off a seat and you land on the deck, walk around while the captain steers (the captain's flippers turn the wheel), or ride standing up.
- **Crosshair, smoother movement, more VFX.** A small crosshair in first person, a touch of slide when you stop, and new effects for chomps, coin fountains, ragdoll impacts, revives, respawns, running on water, flipper flaps, dolphin leaps, hits and the boat's wake.
- **Achievements and titles.** 13 achievements (first catch, beat a boss, bring down the Gull King, dig up treasure, throw a friend, visit every island...). Each one gives a title that shows under your name for everyone; pick it in the AWARDS screen (main menu, **B**) or the pause menu.
- Secrets. A few of them.

## Controls (PC)

New players get a step-by-step tutorial card at the top left (skip with Backspace, replay from **ESC > TUTORIAL**), and **ESC** shows the full controls list. Every key can be changed in **Settings > Controls > Key bindings**; on phones the buttons can be moved, resized or hidden (**Edit buttons**). **L-Alt** frees the mouse.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Left click | Cast / hook / reel |
| Right click | Reel back |
| Q | Throw what you're carrying (or the friend you're holding) |
| E | Pick up / drop / sell to the Walrus / hold to revive a downed friend / talk / cook / board and leave the boat / grab a friend |
| F | Fists out + punch (slap friends, punch the boss) |
| G | Throw a stone |
| X | Eat a fish / use an item |
| 1-6 / Z | Hotbar: rod and bag slots; press the selected slot again for fists |
| L-Alt | Free / lock the mouse |
| B (hold) | Emote wheel |
| I | Fish index |
| Ctrl | Slide |
| Space (tap / hold) | Short / high jump; in the air: flap your flippers; swimming while sprinting: leap out of the water |
| T | First person / third person camera |
| Y | Go ragdoll |
| Menu | Every menu button has a key (O create room, S solo, P pearl shop, D donate, B achievements, F1 settings...). In the lobby: Enter continues, N starts a new game. All keys can be changed in Settings. |
| V | Push-to-talk voice chat |
| Hold R | Back to the stall |

## Playing with friends

Press **CREATE ROOM (online)** and send the room code to your friends, or add them as friends and press **JOIN** next to their name. LAN and solo play work too.

The game is in English by default. You can switch to Turkish (Türkçe) from the menu.
